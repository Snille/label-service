## Traps already solved (see Mimers Brain and cups/Dockerfile)

- The DYMO LW5xx driver needs `autoreconf -fi`, `libboost-dev` and an added `<ctime>` include on Ubuntu 24.04 / GCC 13.
- The CUPS queue must be shared (`printer-is-shared=true`), or CUPS refuses Print-Job from the api container.
- CUPS `Validate-Job` accepts any media name; the service's own format list is the real guard.
- `raster2dymolw_v2` needs a live printer connection, so offline checks stop at CUPS raster (`cupsfilter -m application/vnd.cups-raster`).
- After a roll change the printer may not answer on the network until a label is fed by hand.
