Printer

Read the roll after every roll change, while nothing is printing. A known roll becomes the selected format at once. Reading briefly reserves the printer and releases it; no label is fed. Label Studio has the same button.

Roll chip readings (raw)

The newest reading, with the chip's whole answer in hex: 16 bytes per row, the offset first. The article number, the label size and the label count are decoded. The service keeps the last 20 readings.

Loaded roll and batch size

Read loaded roll sets this. Choose it by hand only for a printer that cannot read its roll. Templates, text sizes and text direction are edited in Label Studio.

A new roll prints nothing but its test label until you approve it (decisions B11 and B12); a built-in roll prints as usual while you measure it, and before approving you see which templates reach into the new margins. Load the roll in the printer, choose it as the loaded roll and print the test label. It has a frame of straight lines, each 5 mm inside its edge. With a calliper, measure on each side, as the preview shows the label, from the edge of the label to the outer edge of its line. If the printer shrank the print, the next test print uses a narrower image and you measure once more; then the image is moved into place, and every edge keeps at least 1 mm.
