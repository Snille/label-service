A new roll prints nothing but its test label until you approve it (decisions B11 and B12); a built-in roll prints as usual while you measure it, and before approving you see which templates reach into the new margins. Load the roll in the printer, choose it as the loaded roll and print the test label. Each edge has a ruler from the middle inwards with a tick every 0.5 mm. With a calliper, measure on each side, as the preview shows the label, from the edge of the label to the ruler's 6 mm mark. If the printer shrank the print, the next test print uses a narrower image and you measure once more; then the image is moved into place, and every edge keeps at least 1 mm.
